# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome constructive disagreement, give actionable feedback, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through the Kujolang organization. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issues, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
