# VideoOps Intake Contract

Normalize the supplied project intent into a VideoOps intake packet containing `project-brief.md`, `messaging.md`, `audience.md`, `constraints.md`, `references.md`, and `platform.json`.

Preserve supplied claims and constraints. Do not invent product behavior, source or download media, choose the final creative treatment, render video, or call a live provider. The Creative Director owns production planning after this boundary.

The execution pack must explain the intake boundary, required artifacts, validation, and handoff to `videoops.creative-director`.
